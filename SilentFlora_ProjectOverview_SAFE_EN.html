01

What is Silent Flora?

Silent Flora is a narrative 3D adventure game built on the Polygon blockchain. It is designed for people who have never interacted with blockchain technology — and who would never seek it out on their own.

The game places players in a quiet forest called the Whispering Grove. Every decision they make changes the world around them. There is no violence. No rush. Only observation, choice and consequence.

Silent Flora is the gateway to blockchain — to discover, understand and reflect. For all generations. No prior knowledge required.

The SFLR token is not a financial product. It is the in-game currency that powers the Silent Flora ecosystem — earned through play, used within the game, and governed by the community.

02

The Problem We Address

Blockchain Remains Inaccessible

Blockchain technology has genuine potential to reshape how people manage identity, ownership and participation in digital systems. Yet for most people, it remains abstract, intimidating and associated with speculation rather than utility.

Tutorials explain it. Whitepapers describe it. But neither approach works for the majority of people who have no existing reason to engage.

Web3 Gaming Has Failed Mainstream Users

Most blockchain games have prioritized token mechanics over gameplay. The result is products that attract speculators but repel everyone else. They require wallet setup before play begins. They promise returns. They speak the language of finance, not fun.

Silent Flora reverses this. The game comes first. The blockchain is invisible until the player is ready to find it.

03

How the Game Works

The Core Experience

The player guides an androgynous wandering figure through the Whispering Grove. The world responds to every decision. Colors shift. A fox reacts — staying or leaving depending on what the player chooses. At the end, the main character dissolves with the last tree.

There is no game over screen. No explanation. The player is left with silence — and whatever they make of it.

Designed for Everyone

Silent Flora requires no gaming experience. No blockchain knowledge. No wallet setup at the start. A player enters their name and begins. Everything else is discovered through play.

Current Status

A fully playable prototype exists, built in Godot Engine 4.6 with custom 3D characters and environments created in Blender. The prototype demonstrates the complete emotional game loop: movement, interaction, decision, world change, and character dissolution.

04

The Learning Journey

Each level of Silent Flora introduces one blockchain concept — not as a lesson, but as a game mechanic. The player experiences it before they understand it. By the time they reach the final level, they have used the fundamental building blocks of blockchain without ever sitting through a tutorial.

1
Wallet and Identity
Enter a name. Start playing.
A wallet is created automatically. The player discovers it later — not as a requirement, but as a feature of the world they are already inhabiting.
2
Token and Value
Decisions earn rewards.
The player earns SFLR tokens through gameplay. Value is created by action — not by purchase. The token represents what the player has done, not what they have spent.
3
Transactions
Use tokens within the world.
Tokens can be used to access new areas or shared with other players. Peer-to-peer exchange becomes natural before it becomes conceptual.
4
Governance
Your voice shapes the world.
Players vote on what happens next in the game world. Community governance is experienced as participation, not as a technical concept.
5
Real-World Application
The question is the goal.
A quiet moment. No instruction. Just the player, what they have experienced, and a space to ask: what can I do with this in real life?
05

The SFLR Token

What SFLR Is

SFLR is a utility token that functions as the in-game currency of Silent Flora. It is earned through gameplay, used to access game content and interact with other players, and governed collectively by the community.

SFLR is not designed as a financial instrument. It does not represent ownership in any company. It does not entitle holders to dividends, profits or any return. Its purpose is to power the Silent Flora game ecosystem.

SFLR is what you earn by playing. It is the record of your participation in the world of Silent Flora — nothing more, nothing less.

How Tokens Are Distributed

The largest share of SFLR — 40% — is reserved for player rewards. Tokens are earned through playing, contributing to the community and supporting the project's growth. The remaining supply covers development, liquidity and early community access.

Built-In Game Mechanics

A small percentage of each in-game transaction is permanently removed from circulation (burned) and a small share supports ongoing development. These mechanics keep the in-game economy balanced over time. They are game design decisions, not financial engineering.
